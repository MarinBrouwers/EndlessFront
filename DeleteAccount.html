Delete Account Request

Package: eu.marin.endlessfront Last reviewed: 30 April 2026

Use this page if you want to request deletion of your Endless Front account and associated cloud data. This page applies to the game published by Marin Brouwers on Google Play.

1. How to request deletion

Send an email to grimportchronicle@gmail.com with the subject line:

Endless Front Account Deletion Request

Please include the Google account email address you used to sign in to Endless Front, so the correct account can be identified.

Send deletion request

2. What will be deleted

If your request is accepted and processed, the developer will delete the cloud data associated with your Endless Front account, including:

  • your player profile row
  • your synced game state / cloud save data
  • your leaderboard entry
  • your authoritative progression state and related command history used to operate the game account

3. What may not be deleted immediately

The developer cannot remotely erase data stored only on your own device. To remove local app data, you should also uninstall the app or clear its data from Android settings.

Minimal security, fraud-prevention, or support records related to the deletion request itself may be kept for up to 30 days after the request is completed, unless a longer retention period is required by law or needed to resolve abuse, chargeback, or security issues.

4. Processing time

Deletion requests are normally reviewed and processed within 30 days.
