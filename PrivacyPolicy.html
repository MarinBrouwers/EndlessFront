Short version: Endless Front is no longer a local-only game. The current version uses Google Sign-In and a Supabase backend to create an account-bound profile, sync game progress, and support leaderboard and integrity features. 2. How the app currently works

Endless Front currently runs as an account-bound game. A Google account sign-in is required to access the live progression path. The app uses:

  • Google Sign-In to let you authenticate with your Google account.
  • Supabase Auth to create and manage your in-game authenticated session.
  • Supabase database tables and RPCs to store player profile data, synced game state, command logs, authoritative state, and leaderboard rows.
  • Local device storage to keep a cached save, settings, and privacy/ad choices on your device.
  • Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads, only if you enable them.

The app does not currently include a separate developer-controlled analytics SDK or crash reporting SDK, but it does process account, save-sync, and leaderboard data through Supabase and related Google sign-in infrastructure.

4. Why this data is used

  • Authentication: to let you sign in with Google and keep you signed in.
  • Cloud progression: to save, restore, and sync your game state.
  • Leaderboard operation: to show ranking data tied to an account.
  • Security and anti-tamper: to review commands, validate state, and protect leaderboard integrity.
  • Local preferences: to remember settings such as privacy acknowledgement and whether rewarded ads are enabled.
  • Rewarded advertising: to load and show optional rewarded ads if you turn that feature on.

5. Rewarded ads and privacy choices

Rewarded ads in Endless Front are optional. On first run, the app asks you to read the privacy policy and choose whether rewarded ads should be available. This choice is stored locally on your device.

If rewarded ads are off: the app does not request rewarded ads from Google AdMob.

If rewarded ads are on: the app may request rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Google may process device and advertising-related data according to Google's own policies and product configuration. You can change this choice later from the in-app privacy options screen.

Rewarded ad-related benefits currently include features such as Supply Surge, Battle Surge, Reinforcements, and Retreat Bonus.

6. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

The main legal bases relied on by the developer are:

  • GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) - performance of a contract / steps at your request: for account sign-in, session handling, cloud save, synced progression, and leaderboard functionality.
  • GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) - legitimate interests: for service security, abuse prevention, anti-tamper review, and maintaining fair leaderboard integrity.

Google may act as an independent controller for parts of Google Sign-In and AdMob processing. For those services, Google's own privacy terms and legal bases also apply.

8. Where data is stored and international transfers

The Endless Front Supabase project is configured in the European region. Supabase states that project data remains within the region selected when the project is created.

Google services used for sign-in and advertising may process data outside Belgium or outside the EEA. If you use Google Sign-In or enable rewarded ads, your data may therefore be transferred through Google systems in accordance with Google's own contractual and legal safeguards.

9. Data retention

Local device data: remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app.

Account, profile, synced save, command, and leaderboard data: is retained in Supabase for as long as your account remains active, and for reasonable backup, integrity, or fraud-review periods as needed to operate the game and investigate abuse.

Rewarded ad data: is retained according to Google's own retention practices.

You should still understand that no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure.
