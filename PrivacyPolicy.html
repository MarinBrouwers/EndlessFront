2. What data is collected and by whom?

The Developer does not collect any personal data. There are no user accounts, no login, no analytics SDK, and no back-end server. All game progress — including unlocked units, currency, and battle records — is saved locally on your device and never leaves it.

The only third party that may process data in connection with this app is Google AdMob (Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA), which provides the rewarded advertisement functionality described below.

3. Rewarded ads and your consent

Endless Front contains optional rewarded video ads. Watching an ad is always your choice — you are never forced to watch one to continue playing. The rewards available through ads are:

  • Supply Surge — bonus resources for your army
  • Battle Surge — a temporary combat boost
  • Field Promotion — an accelerated unit upgrade
  • Retreat Bonus — resources recovered after a tactical retreat

Ads are served by Google AdMob. When you first open the app, a consent screen powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is displayed. This screen explains what data AdMob may use and asks for your permission before any personalised advertising takes place.

If you give consent: Google may use your Advertising ID and other device signals to show ads that are more relevant to you. This is processed under GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) — consent.

If you decline or withdraw consent: Google will serve non-personalised ads instead. Non-personalised ads do not use your Advertising ID for targeting, though Google may still process limited contextual data (such as your IP address and general device type) for ad delivery and fraud prevention, based on its legitimate interests.

You can change your consent choice at any time. On Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads to reset or opt out of ads personalisation, or use the consent management option within the app if available.

4. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

Because the developer does not process personal data directly, the legal bases below apply specifically to Google AdMob's processing as a third-party service operating within the app.

  • Consent — GDPR Art. 6(1)(a): personalised ad targeting using your Advertising ID, collected only when you have given explicit consent through the UMP consent screen.
  • Legitimate interests — GDPR Art. 6(1)(f): basic ad delivery, fraud detection, and non-personalised advertising, where Google has determined these purposes are proportionate and do not override your fundamental rights.

Under Belgian law, the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données — GBA/APD) oversees GDPR enforcement in Belgium. Belgian users may also consult the GBA website for guidance or to file a complaint.

5. International data transfers

Google LLC is based in the United States. When Google AdMob processes data, it may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision, July 2023) and Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission to ensure your data remains protected to EEA standards when transferred internationally.

6. Data retention

Game data (progress, settings): stored locally on your device indefinitely, or until you uninstall the app or clear the app's data through Android's app management settings. The developer has no access to this data and cannot delete it remotely.

Ad-related data: Google AdMob retains advertising data according to its own retention policies. For details, see Google's data retention schedule .
